Full Curriculum Framework — All 4 Phases

Unit-level learning objectives, NCS competency codes, BTEC Unit mapping (NQF Level 3–4), and TAFE SA alignment status across the 12-month roadmap.

P1

Phase 1 · Foundation & Curriculum Localization

Month 1–2 Track ⑤ Curriculum + Track ③ Digital Content 3 Core Units
Learning Objectives
  • Explain the architecture and operating principles of a Programmable Logic Controller (PLC)
  • Write and interpret ladder diagram logic for basic input/output control sequences
  • Configure and troubleshoot a PLC-based conveyor or sorting system
  • Apply IEC 61131-3 programming standards in a structured text environment
  • Demonstrate safe commissioning and de-commissioning procedures per relevant safety standards
NCS Competency Codes
  • NCS 15010201 · 전기제어시스템 설계 (Electrical Control System Design)
  • NCS 15010202 · PLC 프로그래밍 운영 (PLC Programming & Operation)
  • NCS 15010204 · 자동화 시스템 유지보수 (Automation System Maintenance)

Learning Objectives
  • Describe the key components of a smart factory ecosystem: sensors, actuators, SCADA, MES, and ERP integration
  • Monitor and interpret real-time production data from an IoT-enabled manufacturing line
  • Apply predictive maintenance principles using basic data analytics tools
  • Evaluate OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) metrics and propose efficiency improvements
  • Demonstrate cyber-physical system safety and cybersecurity awareness in an industrial network

Learning Objectives
  • Identify and explain the function of core pneumatic components: compressors, valves, actuators, and FRLs
  • Read and interpret ISO 1219-compliant pneumatic circuit diagrams
  • Assemble and test a basic double-acting cylinder circuit using directional control valves
  • Diagnose and resolve common pneumatic faults (leakage, insufficient pressure, valve sticking)
  • Integrate a pneumatic actuator into a PLC-controlled automated sequence

  • NCS 15010102 · 공압회로 구성 및 운영 (Pneumatic Circuit Construction & Operation)
  • NCS 15010105 · 유공압 장치 유지보수 (Hydraulic & Pneumatic Device Maintenance)

Process Stations (8 Modules)
  • Station 1 — Wafer Preparation (substrate cleaning, inspection)
  • Station 2 — Thermal Oxidation (SiO₂ growth, furnace operation)
  • Station 3 — Photolithography (coating, exposure, development)
  • Station 4 — Etching (wet & dry etch, selectivity)
  • Station 5 — Ion Implantation / Doping (dopant profiles)
  • Station 6 — CVD (chemical vapour deposition, film quality)
  • Station 7 — Metallisation (sputtering, interconnect layers)
  • Station 8 — Testing & Packaging (electrical probing, dicing)
Learning Objectives per Station
  • Describe the purpose and parameters of each process step within the IC fabrication sequence
  • Identify equipment used at each station and explain its operating principles
  • Recognise common defect modes and their process origin
  • Navigate the XR environment and complete station-level assessment prompts
