Molding Process — Concepts & Theory
Understand EMC, transfer molding, PID control, cure chemistry, and defect mechanisms
LEARN
🧱 What is Transfer Molding?
Transfer molding encapsulates the die and wire bonds in Epoxy Molding Compound (EMC) to protect them from moisture, mechanical stress, and contamination.

A pre-heated EMC tablet is placed in a pot. A plunger forces liquid EMC through a runner system into closed mold cavities, where it flows around each package and cures under heat and pressure.
⚗️ EMC Cure Chemistry
EMC is a thermoset: once cured, it cannot be re-melted. Cure happens via a cross-linking reaction between epoxy resin and hardener, activated by heat.

Glass Transition Temperature (Tg) — the temperature above which the cured EMC becomes soft. Target Tg is typically 150–180°C. Insufficient cure → low Tg → reliability failure in field.
🌡️ PID Temperature Control
The mold temperature is controlled by a PID controller:
P (Proportional): responds to current error. High Kp → fast response but overshoot.
I (Integral): eliminates steady-state offset. High Ki → eliminates offset but can cause oscillation.
D (Derivative): dampens rate of change. High Kd → reduces overshoot.
📊 SPC and Process Capability
Cpk measures how well the process fits within spec limits, accounting for both spread and centring:
Cpk = min[(USL−μ)/3σ, (μ−LSL)/3σ]

Cpk ≥ 1.33 is the semiconductor industry standard (4σ). ≥ 1.67 is Six Sigma level. Below 1.0 → process is producing defects.

Common Defects — Causes & Fixes
🫧 Void / Air Bubble
Low transfer pressure or high viscosity EMC traps air during fill.
Increase pressure. Extend preheat. Check EMC storage (humidity).
⚡ Wire Sweep
High transfer pressure or fast fill rate deflects fine wires during EMC flow.
Reduce transfer pressure. Use low-α EMC. Check wire loop height.
🌊 Incomplete Fill (Short Shot)
EMC gels before filling cavity — preheat too high, mold temp too high, or slow transfer.
Reduce preheat. Lower mold temp by 5–10°C. Speed up plunger.
📐 Warpage
CTE mismatch between EMC and substrate + non-uniform cooling → package bows.
Optimise cool-down ramp. Use low-stress EMC. Check substrate moisture.
✨ Delamination
Poor adhesion between EMC and die/lead frame due to surface contamination or moisture.
Plasma clean before molding. Dry substrates 125°C/4h. Check adhesion promoter.
💨 EMC Flash
Excess EMC bleeding through parting line — low clamp force or mold wear.
Increase clamp force. Inspect and rework mold parting surface. Adjust EMC shot weight.
