Today’s Meeting Brief

KMSTTH: meeting checklist and time-track action plan

Use this page to keep the conversation disciplined: show interest in principle, verify legal and financial substance, and steer the discussion toward a limited pilot instead of an open-ended commitment.

Meeting stance

Do not treat this as a full partnership decision today. Treat it as a validation meeting: confirm whether KMSTTH has real legal structure, funding credibility, and partner substance, then decide whether a one-country pilot is worth exploring.

Decision lens
What KMSTTH appears to need

IEG’s credibility, execution capability, curriculum, training systems, and track record.

What IEG may gain

Access to new institutional channels and funded pilot opportunities — but only if those channels are real.

Best practical outcome today

Agreement to exchange documents and explore a tightly scoped pilot in a stable Gulf market.

Live meeting checklist

Questions and control points to cover during the call.

Confirm legal substance

Ask whether KMSTTH is already legally incorporated, under which jurisdiction, and in what legal form.

Must confirm today
Ask who is funding Phase 1

Clarify who pays for feasibility, legal setup, and initial operations: founder, investors, donors, or government-linked sponsors.

Separate real partners from aspirational names

Ask which governments, universities, or organizations are already committed and which are still only targets.

Define the exact role expected from IEG

Pin down whether IEG is being asked to serve as advisor, technical partner, or implementation partner.

Clarify geographic scope and exposure

Ask whether the true early focus is UAE / Saudi Arabia / other Gulf markets, and whether Iran is in the near-term operating plan.

Control brand and IP use

State that any use of IEG’s name, logo, curriculum, or institutional profile must require prior written approval.

Push for a pilot-first structure

Say that IEG would be more comfortable exploring one country and one pilot project before discussing broader regional partnership.

Preferred negotiation line

Documents to request right after the meeting

If these do not exist or cannot be shared, that is itself a signal.

Full concept note

A written project concept, not only slides.

Preliminary financial model or budget

At minimum: setup cost, funding assumptions, operating cost, and pilot budget structure.

Legal registration materials

Certificate of incorporation, legal entity details, governance outline, or expected incorporation schedule.

Confirmed partner list

Ask for actual institutions with status labels: confirmed, in negotiation, or target.

Evidence of commitment

LOIs, MOUs, support letters, institutional emails, or other written proof.

Draft scope for IEG involvement

A short note that defines what KMSTTH wants from IEG, by workstream and expected resource level.

Time-track action items

A disciplined path from conversation to decision. Each phase reduces uncertainty before the next one begins.

Immediate · today to this week

Secure facts before signaling any commitment

  • Confirm KMSTTH’s legal status, founder structure, and current stage of establishment.
  • Request the concept note, budget / funding outline, partner list, and any written proof of commitment.
  • Clarify whether IEG is being approached for credibility only, technical content, execution capacity, or all three.
  • State clearly that any next step depends on documentation and internal review.
Short term · next 2 to 4 weeks

Evaluate the proposal and shape a safer structure

  • Review the received documents and test whether partner claims and funding assumptions are credible.
  • Assess geopolitical, compliance, and reputational exposure, especially if Iran appears in the operating scope.
  • Prepare a counter-proposal for a one-country pilot in a more stable Gulf market such as the UAE or Saudi Arabia.
  • Define IEG’s resource cap: number of meetings, staffing level, budget ceiling, and approval pathway.
Medium term · 3 to 6 months

If the concept survives review, move only through a pilot

  • Negotiate a non-binding MOU or framework note with a clear exit clause.
  • Define pilot scope, cost-sharing, deliverables, timeline, and named responsibilities.
  • Bring the opportunity into IEG’s formal internal approval process with supporting documentation.
  • Use the pilot to test whether KMSTTH brings real institutional leverage, not just presentation value.
Long term · after 6 months

Expand only after proof of execution

  • Review pilot outcomes against concrete metrics: funding closed, counterpart engagement, operational delivery, and reputational impact.
  • Consider wider regional expansion only if KMSTTH proves it can convert vision into funded execution.
  • Keep higher-risk markets outside the active plan until geopolitical and compliance conditions improve.

Red lines

  • Do not allow open-ended use of IEG’s name or logo.
  • Do not verbally commit to implementation support without scope, budget, and authority lines.
  • Do not let a slide deck substitute for legal or financial documentation.
  • Do not accept high-risk geography in the near-term plan without formal review.

Best closing message

“We see potential in the concept and would be open to reviewing the concrete materials. From our side, the most practical next step would be to evaluate the documentation and then explore whether there is a well-defined pilot opportunity that makes sense for both parties.”
