Executive Summary

IEG recommends a Phase 1 decision focused on curriculum validation, instructor readiness, and institutional alignment before ALAT/TVTC commits to major cleanroom infrastructure. This creates a measurable evidence base for Phase 2 procurement and reduces the risk of under-utilised facilities.

ALAT/TVTC Decision Need
  • Confirm a semiconductor TVET curriculum suitable for Saudi delivery
  • Prepare instructors before capital equipment deployment
  • Define a credible Model C lab specification from actual training evidence
IEG Phase 1 Response
  • Deliver BTEC-mapped, TVTC-aligned online curriculum assets
  • Run Korea-based TTT for 5-10 Saudi instructors
  • Produce a TTT Pilot Report and Phase 2 cleanroom concept brief

Core Principle: Curriculum and Instructor Readiness Before Hardware

IEG's model places online learning, assessment design, and Train-the-Trainer delivery before major equipment procurement. By the time ALAT/TVTC evaluates the Model C Cleanroom, Saudi instructors will have completed structured preparation, the curriculum will be locally adapted, and the laboratory specification will be supported by Phase 1 evidence rather than assumptions.

Why Online-First Reduces Implementation Risk

The central delivery risk in a semiconductor TVET centre is not only equipment procurement; it is whether instructors, curriculum, assessment, and facility planning are ready at the same time. Phase 1 is designed to synchronise those components before Phase 2 investment.

1A

Online Curriculum Package — Delivered Remotely, Month 1–2

IEG delivers BTEC-mapped semiconductor curriculum assets as bilingual-ready digital content: an 8-process interactive simulator, asynchronous module scripts, competency self-assessments, and a TVTC occupational standard crosswalk. Saudi instructors can begin structured preparation without immediate travel or hardware dependency.

USD 20,000 · 100% remote delivery · No equipment required
1B

Korea Technology Tour (TTT Pilot) — 5–7 Days, IEG HQ Seongnam

5-10 ALAT/TVTC instructors visit IEG headquarters to operate PLC, MPS, pneumatics, and smart-factory training systems comparable to those proposed for Saudi deployment. The programme includes Korea Polytechnic University engagement, industry site exposure, assessment calibration, and a co-design workshop for Saudi-localised delivery.

USD 27,500 · Includes training fees + accommodation + airport transfer (5–10 pax)
2

Model C Cleanroom Proposal — Submitted at Month 11

Using the TTT Pilot Report, IEG submits a Model C Cleanroom concept package for ALAT/TVTC review. The package includes equipment list, layout assumptions, commissioning plan, instructor utilisation model, and financing pathways such as EDCF, Saudi-Korea bilateral funding, or direct commercial procurement.

Pricing TBD · EDCF / bilateral financing recommended
3–4

Operational Scale-Up — Year 2 Onwards

Once the Model C centre is commissioned, IEG provides annual instructor refresher training, XR digital twin content updates, and curriculum review cycles. The ALAT centre becomes a regional reference site, enabling expansion to additional ALAT locations across Saudi Arabia.

Annual maintenance contract · Optional: replication to other ALAT sites

IEG Recommendations

Direct answers to ALAT/TVTC's three supplementary questions, stated as implementation recommendations for a staged semiconductor TVET partnership.

⭐ Priority 1: Which laboratories should be prioritised for Phase 1?

IEG recommends that ALAT/TVTC does not prioritise a new laboratory build in Phase 1. The higher-value first step is to validate curriculum, assessment, and instructor readiness through Phase 1A online delivery and Phase 1B Korea-based TTT. This produces trained instructors, localised curriculum evidence, and a TTT Pilot Report that can guide the optimal Model C configuration for Saudi workforce requirements.

⭐ Priority 2: Which digital content should ALAT/TVTC deploy immediately?

The IEG Semiconductor 8-Process Interactive Simulator can be used for ALAT/TVTC internal evaluation as an early diagnostic tool. IEG recommends distributing it to selected instructors and engineering trainers to establish baseline familiarity with semiconductor process flow, terminology, and operational decisions. Their feedback will help calibrate Phase 1A content to the correct starting level for Saudi delivery.

⭐ Priority 3: Minimum investment to launch Phase 1

USD 20,000 launches Phase 1A and provides a concrete evaluation package: BTEC mapping table, TVTC alignment notes, interactive simulator, and 8 video module scripts. The full Phase 1 package is USD 47,500 and adds the Korea TTT pilot for 5-10 instructors, including certificates, co-design workshop outputs, and a Phase 2 concept brief.

The Core Argument for Phase 1 Approval

ALAT/TVTC is being asked to invest USD 47,500 in Phase 1. In return, by Month 7: (1) a BTEC-mapped and TVTC-aligned curriculum package is available for review, (2) 5-10 Saudi instructors have completed structured TTT and operated relevant IEG training equipment, (3) a TTT Pilot Report is ready to inform the Phase 2 cleanroom concept, and (4) ALAT/TVTC has evidence to decide whether, when, and how to proceed with hardware procurement. The hardware decision is therefore based on validated curriculum and instructor readiness.
